01 · The problem

Today we rent our reach.

Clients come to us for exposure. We go to other people's pages to get it. That works, but it caps how far we can grow.

Rented

We buy posts on pages other people own. They set the price, they keep the audience, and they can say no.

Manual

Every campaign is negotiated, made and posted by hand. More clients means more of our hours.

Unproven

We have no live numbers to show a new client, so every sale starts from zero trust.

What if we owned the pages, the content machine and the proof?

06 · The money

Seven ways the attention pays.

One engine feeds every line. The Exposure Network brings cash first because it sells reach on pages we already built. Prices are starting ideas to test in the pilot.

Exposure Network

$100–400 per campaign · $500–1,500 / month retainers

The "one-click" product. A client picks niches, pages and dates; the campaign runs natively across pages we own; a live report link shows views and clicks.

Creator Network

margin on paid views

Real micro-creators in Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Thailand and the Philippines post campaigns on their own accounts, paid per verified 1,000 views. Never charge them to join.

Community Hub

sponsored posts · services

Value-first Facebook Groups such as "F-commerce Sellers BD", where members promote themselves under fair rules, and buy our marketing services.

Creator & Artist Management

fee + % of deals

Take real and AI talent from zero to known, or push existing creators onto new platforms for a revenue share. AI talent is always labelled as AI.

Music Division

royalties · tickets · promo packs

Genre pages, a label, song-promotion packs seeded through hundreds of creators, and a collective that grows from club nights to festivals.

Education

courses · workshops

Free AI and digital-skills tutorials build trust. Paid mini-courses follow. The best students join the Creator Network.

Payouts & affiliate

platform revenue · commissions

YouTube and Facebook payouts, product affiliate links, and only regulated financial products.

Upgrades, best value first

  1. RAM 16 → 32 GB · $60–100. AI models spill into system RAM.
  2. UPS · $80–150. Load-shedding kills overnight renders.
  3. Ubuntu dual-boot · free. AMD AI tools behave far better on Linux.
  4. Used RTX 3090 24 GB · $650–800, at Scale. Local video and AI-talent training.

09 · Red lines
